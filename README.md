# Dailydrop-Ecommerce-Sales-Dashboard
An interactive Power BI sales dashboard analyzing revenue, profitability, order quantities, customer contribution, and payment trends across states and product categories.

Key Metrics -
Total Revenue: 438K
Total Profit: 37K
Total Quantity Sold: 5,615 units
Average Order Value: 121K

Key Insights -
1. Maharashtra and Madhya Pradesh generate the highest sales amounts.
2. Clothing drives the highest sales quantity (63%).
3. Cash on Delivery (COD) dominates at 44%.
4. Printers and Bookcases yield the highest profit, while Tables and Accessories show the lowest margins.
5. Profit peaks in Q1 (Jan–Mar) and November, with sharp margin dips/losses observed in May and July.
6. Harivansh, Madhav, and Madan Mohan are the leading individual revenue contributors.

Final Conclusion -
Business revenue is heavily sustained by high-volume clothing sales and COD orders in Maharashtra and Madhya Pradesh, while bottom-line profitability relies on high-margin items like printers and bookcases amidst mid-year seasonal dips.
